05 / Binding

Binding Methods

The binding method affects how a product opens, feels in use and performs over time.

Different formats, page counts and use cases require different binding solutions.

01
Saddle Stitching
Wire staples through the spine fold. Optimal for 8–80 pages. Lays flat when open, highly cost-effective at volume.
Booklets · Catalogues
02
Perfect Binding
Signatures glued to a square spine. Standard for paperbacks and magazines above 80 pages.
Paperbacks
03
Case Binding
Sewn or glued blocks attached to a rigid hardcover. The benchmark for permanence and craft.
Hardcovers
04
Wire-O Binding
Double-loop wire through punched holes. Rotates 360° and opens completely flat — preferred for notebooks and reference documents that must stay open hands-free.
Notebooks · Reference
05
PUR Binding
Polyurethane reactive adhesive. Superior flex and heat resistance versus standard hotmelt.
High-durability
06
Bolt Binding
Metal screws through punched stock. Pages can be added or removed without rebinding.
Portfolios · Loose-leaf
07
Thread Sewing
Signatures sewn with thread before casing. Maximum durability — used for museum-quality and archival editions.
Archival · Fine editions
