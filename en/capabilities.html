01Production Space

Production in plain sight.

Open-space production

Office and production work side by side within one connected space. Here you can see the team at work, equipment, materials and every stage of production — from artwork preparation and printing to print finishing, binding, packing and delivery.

Large street-facing windows make the production process visible even from outside. We like keeping the process open and easy to understand.

We're here to help.

Printing involves many materials, printing technologies and finishing options that are not always easy to navigate. It is just as important to understand how they work together and what result they create in the finished product.

Every project is managed by print production specialists. They help select materials, printing methods and finishing options based on the project, budget and timeline.

02Print

Printing Technologies

Every printing technology has its strengths. The choice depends on the material, print run and how the finished product is meant to look and perform.

01

Digital Printing

CMYK / White Ink

Ideal for short and medium runs, personalised names, barcodes, QR codes and other variable data, as well as urgent orders. White Ink is used on transparent, dark and coloured materials — either as a standalone colour or as a base layer for full-colour CMYK printing. Black & white digital printing provides fast production of large volumes of documents, manuals, training materials, books and technical documentation.

Capabilities
  • Full-colour CMYK printing
  • White Ink printing
  • Black & white printing
  • Variable data & personalisation
  • Short and medium runs
  • Urgent orders
02

Offset Printing

CMYK / Pantone / Spot Colours

Best for medium and large print runs where the cost per copy becomes lower as quantities increase. Pantone inks are used for accurate brand colours, smooth solid colour areas, and colours that cannot be reproduced in CMYK — including fluorescent, pastel and metallic shades (gold, silver, bronze and more). Colour remains clean, even and consistent from the first sheet to the last.

Capabilities
  • Full-colour CMYK printing
  • Pantone printing
  • Accurate brand colours
  • Fluorescent, pastel & metallic colours
  • Smooth solid colour areas
  • Medium & large print runs
03

UV Printing

CMYK / White Ink / Clear UV / 3D Effect

Designed for direct printing on promotional products, acrylic and other rigid materials. White Ink makes it possible to print on transparent, dark and coloured surfaces, and can be applied in one or multiple layers to create a raised effect. Spot UV Varnish can also be flat or raised, highlighting selected design elements.

Capabilities
  • Full-colour UV CMYK printing
  • White Ink printing
  • Spot UV Varnish
  • Raised printing
  • Acrylic & rigid materials
  • Dark & transparent materials
